Rendering rules
Evidence
Teal count badge on each node; zero-badge nodes still render.
Confidence
3 px bar under the label — teal ≥ 0.85, amber 0.70–0.85, rose < 0.70.
Finding
Dashed rose outline plus a flag label; never colour alone.
Heat
Optional amber fill for effort or value; legend required when active.
Selection
Blue 2.2 px stroke with a soft halo; selection survives overlay toggles.
